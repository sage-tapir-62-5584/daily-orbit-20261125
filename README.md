Entry for make.fun's bounty **"Sum up 25 November's world news in one image"**
(slug `news-image-20261125`).

The entry image is a one-page newspaper poster that sums up a single day —
**Wednesday, 25 November 2026**, named in large type under the masthead. It
carries one lead story, five further stories across regions and fields, and
an "Also that day" strip:

- **Lead:** two oil tankers hit naval mines in the Strait of Hormuz and
  catch fire, Iran's Revolutionary Guards say; more vessels reported hit
  later in the day.
- The Houthis claim a strike on Ras Tanura, Saudi Arabia's biggest refinery.
- Pakistan's first astronaut, Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, boards China's Tiangong
  space station aboard Shenzhou-24.
- Solomon Islands signs a treaty with Australia barring any foreign military
  base on its soil.
- The US proposes new tariffs on 14 trading partners, China facing the
  highest rate at 20%.
- RSF drone strikes kill 19 civilians in Sudan's Blue Nile state.
- Also that day: an Israeli strike kills Hamas security chief Abuelouf and
  his family in Gaza; Pope Leo calls the failure of COP31 "troubling";
  Bangladesh raises garment workers' minimum wage by 36%.

Every story happened, or was first reported, on 25 November 2026 and was
confirmed from at least two independent outlets; see **SOURCES.md** for the
story-by-story sourcing.

## How the image was made

The poster is drawn in code, not generated by an image model, so every word
is spelled exactly as written. `poster/poster.html` is the page template,
`poster/news-data.json` holds the stories as data, and the flat illustrations
are hand-drawn inline SVG. `poster/render.mjs` fills the template from the
data and screenshots it in headless Chromium at exactly 1024×1024, producing
`assets/entry.png` (PNG, 1024×1024, ~190 KB).

## Running the entry

```
python3 main.py                      # writes /output/entry.png
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py   # or a directory of your choice
```

`main.py` uses only the Python standard library, needs no network, and
copies `assets/entry.png` byte-for-byte, so repeated runs always produce an
identical file.
