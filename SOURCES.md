## Secondary stories

**Houthis claim a strike on Saudi Arabia's biggest refinery, Ras Tanura**
- Reuters, via The Straits Times (25 Nov 2026) — "Yemen's Houthis claim
  attack on Saudi Arabia's largest refinery": the Houthis said they had
  attacked Ras Tanura; satellite images from NASA and Planet Labs showed
  thick black smoke; a source confirmed an attack. The Saudi-led coalition
  said air defences intercepted four drones over Ras Tanura.
- Xinhua (25 Nov 2026) — "Yemen's Houthis say attacked Saudi oil refinery,
  financial center": Houthi spokesman Yahya Sarea said the Ras Tanura
  operation caused fires, and a second operation targeted a financial
  centre in Riyadh. Channel News Asia (25 Nov 2026) reported the same
  claims and the resulting rise in oil prices.

**Pakistan's first astronaut reaches China's Tiangong space station**
- Xinhua (25 Nov 2026) — "China launches Shenzhou-24 spaceship, opening
  Tiangong space station to first foreign astronaut": Shenzhou-24 launched
  at 08:18 Beijing time with Tang Hongbo, Wu Yi and Pakistani payload
  specialist Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, "the first foreign astronaut to board
  Tiangong".
- Reuters, via Arab News (25 Nov 2026) — "China's Shenzhou-24 space flight
  takes off with Pakistani national as first foreign astronaut"; Channel
  News Asia (25 Nov 2026) — the station "welcomed its first foreign lodger
  on Wednesday (Nov 25)".

**Solomon Islands signs a treaty with Australia barring foreign bases**
- Al Jazeera (25 Nov 2026) — "Solomon Islands signs security deal with
  Australia": the treaty affirms "no foreign military bases on its
  territory", makes Australia the "primary security partner", and commits
  A$740 million over 10 years.
- AFP, via The Korea Herald (25 Nov 2026) — "Australia, Solomon Islands
  sign sweeping pact stymieing China": the treaty was signed on Wednesday
  in Canberra by prime ministers Matthew Wale and Anthony Albanese. The
  Wall Street Journal (25 Nov 2026) and Nikkei Asia reported the same
  signing and terms.

**US proposes new tariffs on 14 trading partners**
- Reuters (25 Nov 2026) — "US proposes new tariffs on 14 trading partners
  to fight excess factory capacity": the Trump administration proposed new
  tariffs on 14 major trading partners over "structural excess industrial
  capacity", with the highest rates to hit China (a proposed 20%
  excess-capacity tariff).
- Al Jazeera (25 Nov 2026) — "Trump announces new trade tariffs: Which
  countries are affected?": the USTR announced the duties on Wednesday
  after an eight-month Section 301 investigation; China faces the highest
  rates. Kitco / The Daily Star (25 Nov 2026) reported the same
  announcement and rates.

**RSF drone strikes kill 19 civilians in Sudan's Blue Nile state**
- Reuters (25 Nov 2026) — "Drone strikes kill 19 civilians in southeastern
  Sudan, says rights group": drone strikes by the Rapid Support Forces
  killed 19 civilians and wounded 20 in Blue Nile state on Tuesday night,
  Sudanese rights group Emergency Lawyers said on Wednesday; 19 died in
  Damazin, five more were wounded in Roseires.
- Al Jazeera (25 Nov 2026) — "Sudan's RSF kills at least 19 in drone
  attacks on Blue Nile state": at least 19 killed in Damazin late on
  Tuesday, at least 20 wounded, according to Emergency Lawyers and a
  Sudanese military source.
